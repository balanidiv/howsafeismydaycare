Methodology

Public records, one search box.

Every facility page links to that operation’s official record on Search Texas Child Care. This site queries the same HHSC Child Care Licensing data live (via the Texas Open Data Portal) and adds a derived grade so you can scan a file quickly.

Where the data comes from

Texas Health and Human Services Commission, Child Care Licensing, via the Texas Open Data Portal (Socrata). No scrape. No third-party grade vendor.

  • Search Texas Child Care — official parent-facing HHSC portal
  • Operations — license, capacity, status, deficiency counts
  • Inspections / investigations / assessments
  • Non-compliance (deficiencies)

Included operation types: Licensed Center, Licensed Child-Care Home, Registered Child-Care Home, Listed Family Home. Excluded: general residential operations and child-placing agencies.

How the letter grade is computed

This is not an official HHSC rating. It is a parent-perspective score for Texas files only. The letter is absolute, not a ranking: A means no High in the last 24 months, F means an open High (both corrected_date and date_correction_verified missing). A due date without verification is not treated as open. Clean cities will show a wall of A’s — that is the honest answer, not a failure of the scale. Recency is the inspection/incident date (joined on activity_id), not the correction date. A corrected finding weighs one-quarter of an open finding at the same risk level. Search cards and facility pages share the same HHSC math. Google is a separate exhibit and is not part of the letter.

The mix — safety and inspections — is shown on the facility page so the file is readable. It does not restamp the letter. Google sits in its own labeled exhibit and does not change those numbers.

Google reviews

Search cards and facility pages may show a live Google exhibit: a rating, stars, and on the facility page up to five review quotes. Those reviews are not saved. They are not a substitute for the HHSC file, and they do not change the letter or the mix numbers.

Limits

  • Texas only. This ship does not include any other state.
  • A clean file is not a promise. Inspectors miss things. Directors fix things after the visit.
  • Do not use this instead of a tour, references, or your state licensing office.
